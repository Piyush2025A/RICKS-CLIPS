# Separated body parts sheet (for rigging cut-out cards)

## Prompt
Cut-out puppet parts for the ORIGINAL cartoon character "{{character_name}}", matching the attached approved turnaround
({{reference_asset_id}}), front view only. Every part drawn separately, complete (including the hidden area where it would
overlap its neighbour, with a rounded joint end), widely spaced on a flat uniform neutral grey (#808080) background so
that none touch or overlap: head without face features, torso, pelvis, upper arm x2, forearm x2, {{hand_style}} hand x2,
thigh x2, shin x2, foot x2, headgear {{headgear}}. Parts at the same scale as the full character (height about 3.3 head
heights, leg about 1.4 times torso). Left and right parts mirrored copies are acceptable.
Style: flat 2D cut-out, soft gradient shading with no cel bands, clean uniform {{ink_colour}} outline about 3.7 percent of
head width baked in on every part including the joint ends, at most 6 colours, no cast shadow, no text, no labels.
Original design only. Do not imitate any existing character or franchise. No real people, brands, text or watermark text.
Avoid: parts touching, overlapping parts, perspective, joints drawn with seams or circles, fingers, background gradient.

## Checklist
all listed parts present once or twice as stated | grey gutter around each part (for keying) | grey does not appear in the palette |
outline closed on each part | scale consistent | no text
Note: the grey background is a keying aid. The model is not guaranteed to output transparency; A24 decides alpha handling and
records any cut-out as a derived asset.
