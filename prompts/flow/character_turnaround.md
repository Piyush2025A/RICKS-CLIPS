# Character turnaround sheet (front, three-quarter, side, back)

## Prompt
Character model sheet of one ORIGINAL cartoon character "{{character_name}}": {{one_line_role_and_age_group}}.
Four full-body views in a single horizontal row on a flat uniform neutral grey (#808080) background, same scale, same
ground line, evenly spaced, no overlap: front view, three-quarter view, side view, back view. Neutral standing pose, arms
slightly away from the body, relaxed expression.
Construction: {{head_shape}} head with width to height ratio of about 1 to 0.93; total height about 3.3 head heights;

feet; {{hand_style}} hands without fingers. Headgear/hair: {{headgear}}. Tertiary mark: {{tertiary_mark}}.
Palette (at most 6 colours): {{palette}}. Skin/face area near white unless stated: {{face_colour}}.

percent of head width, outline baked into the artwork, no scenery, no cast shadow, no text.
Original design only. Do not imitate any existing character, mascot or franchise. No real people, brands, text or watermark text.
Avoid: realistic anatomy, fingers, 3D render look, hatching, textured paper, multiple characters, props not listed, labels.
