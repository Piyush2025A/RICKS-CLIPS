# Storytelling & retention bible (draft v0.1)

| # | Rule | Value | Evidence | Conf |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| S4 | Gap readable sound-off via in-world text/counter | ≈ 20/30 | F-a19-004, F-a18-008 | M |

| S7 | Pivots: first ≈ 10–18 s; "So"/"But"/"Wait" heads beats every ≈ 10–15 s | — | F-a19-007, F-a18-004 | L–M |

| S9 | Endings: concept named / ironic flip / reaction close; loop/callback to frame 0 in 11/30 | — | F-a19-009/010 | M |

| S11 | Third person, past tense story, present tense mechanism; "you" rare | 1/8 sustained | F-a18-005 | L–M |
| S12 | Facts dramatised by ordinary characters acting consequences + short quoted lines read by the narrator | 6/8 | F-a18-006 | M |

| S14 | Production fit | ≤ 6 reusable sets per story | F-a17-009 | M |
