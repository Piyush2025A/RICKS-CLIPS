# A19 TECHNIQUES — hook and retention recipes for ORIGINAL scripts and storyboards

Status: derived from FINDINGS (F-a19-001..012), Phase 3 RC30, medium confidence; all retention effects are HYPOTHESIS until tested on our own analytics. Study craft; never reuse reference stories, jokes or captions.

## T1 Opening rules (from F-a19-001/002/006)
1. Narration word 1 at 0.0-0.5 s; caption on from word 1; no title card, no logo, no greeting.
2. Frame 0 is a posed scene: a character mid-action OR the single key object in macro (only if the object is the question).
3. Title noun visible by 3.0 s (shot 1 or 2). Exception: when the title noun is the answer (delay to the reveal).
4. One burned-in word-or-phrase caption at a time, lower third (~70% of frame height), 1-3 words, so the gap reads sound-off.

## T2 Hook formulas (one per archetype; fill the slots with ORIGINAL facts)
- H1 Claim: "[Everyone assumes RULE]. [Subject] did the opposite and [result]." (0-3 s) Image: subject doing the opposite action.
- H2 Mystery object/act: "[Character] does [odd action] with [object] every [time]." No explanation. Image: macro of object then character.
- H3 Paradox: "[A] is guaranteed. Yet [B] keeps happening." Image: both A and B in one wide shot with a readable sign/counter.
- H4 Stakes: "[Number] [threat] by [deadline], and [subject] has [flawed tool]." Image: scale shot (crowd, tower, ocean) then the tool.
- H5 In-progress event: open on the incident (impact, dig, spoken line), caption starts mid-sentence, context in sentence 2.
- H6 Direct address: "Imagine you [situation with a constraint]." Image: character stands in for the viewer, camera at their eye level. Use sparingly (2/30 in reference).
- H7 Question: ask it in the character's voice, answer withheld until 55-70%.
Rule: hook sentence <= 12 words; the paradox/surprise lands inside the first 3 s of speech; write 3 variants per production (different archetypes), pick one, log rationale in HOOKS.md.

## T3 Gap map (write for every script)
Columns: gap id, opens at sentence N / t, partial answer (theory or complication) at t, true answer at t, closes at t.
Targets for a 60-65 s short: gap 1 opens 0-3 s; first pivot ("But"/"So"/"Wait"/"Still") at 10-18 s; complication 2 at 30-40 s; turn/mechanism reveal at 55-70% (33-45 s); payoff and button at last 8-15 s. Max 2 open gaps at once.

## T4 Beat sheet for 30-80 s Shorts (second marks; scale by duration D)
| Beat | 30-40 s | 55-65 s | 70-80 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (gap 1 opens; first image) | 0-3 | 0-3 | 0-4 |
| Situation + rule (who, where, what is at stake) | 3-9 | 3-12 | 4-14 |
| First pivot / problem tightens ("But", "Still") | ~10 | 12-18 | 14-22 |
| Escalation: 2-3 attempts or complications (change set/lighting each) | 10-20 | 18-40 | 22-50 |
| Re-hook / turn (in-character "Wait," or a new fact) | ~20 | 33-40 | 40-52 |
| Mechanism reveal (diagram/prop with text) | 20-27 | 40-52 | 52-65 |
| Payoff + consequence | 27-D-3 | 52-D-4 | 65-D-5 |
| Button (title concept in last 1-2 captions, gag or callback) | last 3 | last 4 | last 5 |
Check: counters/signs for each gap; a visual change at least every 2-3 s (reference quick-cut openers: shot length 0.8-1.2 s in first 3 s).

## T5 Opening shot templates (to A14/A16)
- B1 Quick-cut opener: 3 shots in 3 s: object/macro -> character reacting -> wide establishing; durations 1.0/1.0/1.0 s; caption continuous.
- B2 Stage opener: 1 set, slow dolly-in (3-6% scale over 3 s) while the character acts out one beat per second; first cut or dissolve after 3.5-4 s (reference: RC30-25 3.97 s, RC30-30 3.83 s).

## T6 Ending/loop patterns
- E1 Strict loop: last 1.0 s = same camera + character position as frame 0, one state changed (expression, prop count, counter value); last caption reuses an opening word. Loop-score = yes.
- E2 Prop callback: return the opening prop/counter transformed (e.g. a "days since" counter reset, a sign changed).
- E3 Title payoff: final caption word is the title concept (never before the last 2 s if the title is a question).
- E4 Gag button: a one-beat visual joke after the payoff (use originally motivated gags only).
- E5 Ironic counter flip: a number on a sign changes meaning in the last beat.
Pick one primary (E1 or E2 for loop potential) plus E3.

## T7 Sound-off clarity checklist
Every open gap has an in-world readable prop; the word that carries the surprise is the one on screen at the moment of the visual change; no gap depends on audio-only sarcasm; captions never occlude the prop that carries the gap (keep prop in upper two thirds).

## T8 Review checklist for A18/A14 drafts (retention risk)
- Time-to-surprise > 3 s of speech: risk.
- No visual change for > 3 s: risk.
- Two gaps closed at the same sentence (flat stretch): risk.
- Reveal before 45% of runtime: risk of a long flat tail (HYPOTHESIS).
- First pivot after 20 s: risk of sag.
- Ending without title payoff word or loop: weaker end (HYPOTHESIS).
